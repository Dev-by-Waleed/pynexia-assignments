Profile

Planning that feels effortless — and runs on schedule.

For nine years I've orchestrated weddings and commercial events with one obsession: the guest experience. My approach combines detailed production timelines, calm on-site leadership, and a design language that always reflects the couple — or the brand — behind the day.

Whether it's a black-tie ballroom reception or a product launch for 800 guests, I own the full lifecycle: concept and budgeting, vendor negotiation, logistics and run-of-show, and the post-event wrap report.

  • Full-service planning, partial planning & day-of coordination
  • Commercial events: galas, conferences, launches, award nights
  • Budget stewardship with transparent weekly reporting
  • Contingency and risk planning built into every timeline
